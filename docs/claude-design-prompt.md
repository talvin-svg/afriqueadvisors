Design a dark-theme marketing website for Afrique Advisors Canada Inc., a boutique advisory firm run by Frank Tawiah, MBA, CFA. Design only: no code yet. The designs will be handed to a developer to build in Next.js + Tailwind CSS, so keep everything systematic and buildable.

# The business

Afrique Advisors Canada Inc. is an Ontario-based management and financial consulting firm founded in 2021. It helps Canadian small businesses and international companies set up, stay compliant, keep clean books, file their taxes and plan for growth in Canada. It has a sister firm in Accra, Ghana (Core Afrique Investment Ltd, also led by Frank) and positions itself as a bridge between Canadian and African markets.

Tagline: "Practical consulting for small businesses building in Canada"

Website goal: win advisory clients. Within 5 seconds a visitor should understand who Frank helps, trust his credentials, and see a clear "Book a consultation" action.

Audiences:
1. Canadian small businesses: owners who need dependable bookkeeping, corporate tax filing and annual compliance handled properly.
2. International & offshore companies: firms entering Canada that need an entity set up, a resident director and local market research.
3. Founders & growing SMEs: entrepreneurs preparing business plans, financial models and funding or grant applications.

Feel: trustworthy, senior, financially rigorous, warm rather than cold-corporate, with a confident nod to African heritage. It should look like a premium independent advisor, not a big bank and not a crypto startup.
